### About me

I am an IT professional passionate about open-source projects and process management, specializing in streamlining business operations through technology. With over 20 years of experience, I began my career as a Web Developer—notably migrating legacy ASP sites to JSP and building custom CMS and project management tools.

My background includes implementing J2EE solutions using POJOs and design patterns to develop complex auditing systems, communication platforms, and digital workpapers. I also spent two years in the mainframe environment working with COBOL, Natural, and JCL to support accounting systems and batch processing.

Most recently, I have focused on deploying open-source tools, serving as a SysAdmin, SRE, and Perl Programmer. I also bring hands-on experience in Data Engineering, including ETL processes and multidimensional modeling (OLAP).
